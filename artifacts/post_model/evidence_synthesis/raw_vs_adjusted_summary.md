# Raw versus adjusted association summary

Adjustment uses the frozen training partition and selected observed covariates with match-cluster-robust standard errors. It does not remove all confounding.

- **Shen**: 65.8% top-four, +15.8 pp, n=8,199; raw OR 2.27; adjusted OR 1.84 (95% CI 1.66–2.04). The positive direction persisted after selected-covariate adjustment and Shen also had mapped TreeSHAP evidence.
- **Aurora**: 38.8% top-four, −11.2 pp, n=2,387; raw OR 0.62; no directly mapped adjusted estimate because Aurora was not among the prespecified common-unit regression terms.
- **Force Of Nature**: 77.0% top-four, +27.0 pp, n=331; raw OR 3.39; no directly mapped adjusted estimate. Its high raw uplift should be interpreted alongside relatively modest support and survivorship/item-acquisition bias.
- **Space Groove · tier 4 · 7 units**: 70.8% top-four, +20.8 pp, n=1,222; raw OR 2.49; no directly mapped adjusted estimate because the adjusted model used a limited set of common trait signatures.
- **Blitzcrank**: 61.0% top-four, +11.0 pp, n=9,300; raw OR 1.79; adjusted OR 1.94 (95% CI 1.73–2.17).
- **Morgana**: 60.8% top-four, +10.8 pp, n=10,303; raw OR 1.80; adjusted OR 1.79 (95% CI 1.65–1.94).
- **Madreds Bloodrazor**: 57.8% top-four, +7.8 pp, n=14,790; raw OR 1.65; adjusted OR 1.28 (95% CI 1.19–1.37), showing material association attenuation after adjustment.
- **Frozen Heart**: 56.7% top-four, +6.7 pp, n=12,714; raw OR 1.49; adjusted OR 1.07 (95% CI 0.99–1.15), showing strong attenuation and no adjusted q<.05 evidence.

Attenuation is labelled association attenuation after covariate adjustment and is not interpreted as confounding explained.
